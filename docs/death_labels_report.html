Where the death head's labels come from

snakeNN, 2026-08-21. Every number below was produced by a run named beside it.

The question. The death head predicts, per action, how likely that move is to kill the snake. It can only be trained where the search measured every root action - and the search measures 1.14 of 3. So the head learns from about four percent of the positions it is shown.

What would make this the wrong framing: assuming the answer is more coverage. That is one of two routes and it is the one that costs play strength.

The state, measured

AlphaZeroCoverage on az10_death368.pt, 10x10, 300 positions sampled evenly along two held-out games, 200 simulations. Cost 573.05s.

Two of three actions are survivable in 49.14 percent of positions and all three in 24.28 percent, so the missing visits are on real alternatives rather than on walls.

Every term of PUCT's exploration half multiplies the prior, so a prior near zero yields an exploration term near zero. Sweeping c_puct a hundredfold moved coverage from 1.13 to 1.24 actions. No constant recovers it.

Applied at every node it destroys play

Self-play fell from 97.5 apples to 3.8 with no wins, degrading between iterations, while the labels did arrive at 126 of 128 per batch. The mix weight is eps |A| / log(N + 1) in the node's own visit count: 0.057 at a root with 200 visits, 0.433 at a node with one - and 200 simulations leave most of the tree at one visit.

Restricted to the root it is free, in coverage terms

descentMixWeight returns zero below depth 0. Root-only at epsilon 0.1 reaches 0.9900 where the floor at every node reached 0.9967, so the deep half was buying nothing.

Coverage condition met. The score condition - self-play back near baseline rather than 3.8 - is not yet measured. That is one training run.

The finding that guts it. 87 x 0.229885 = 20.0. 2405 x 0.008316 = 20.0. The widened rule multiplies the labels 27.6-fold and adds not one additional doomed action. Every positive example already sits inside the fully-covered positions; the extra 2318 pairs are all negatives.

For a head whose job is to flag rare death, more negatives is not the scarce thing.

That also explains the rank correlation falling from 0.729106 to 0.157291: the 87 pairs come from fully-covered positions, which are the positions the head was trained on, so 0.729106 is close to in-sample. Spread 0.109950 still clears the 0.012023 floor nine times over, so the head does say something everywhere - just much less.

Two cautions on these numbers

  • The AUC of 1.000000 rests on 20 positives. Perfect separation on 20 is thin evidence.
  • The floor is not position-paired - the untrained network played its own games. Its coverage is telling anyway: 2439 kept, 0 rejected. A network with uniform priors covers every root action, which confirms saturation as the cause from the opposite direction.

What the literature settled, and what it did not

Willemsen, Baier and Kaisers 2020, "Value targets in off-policy AlphaZero"

Sorts four AlphaZero value targets into one family by three numbers: how far to bootstrap in the real game, how far in the simulated tree, and whether a backup averages the subtree or takes a single sample.

A one-visit backup is not noise. Their best target, A0GB, backs up from a node with exactly one visit and wins - it is the only one of four that picks the right move in every state of a tabular gridworld after 40,000 games. So a single visit is a variance claim, not a validity claim. Our forced root child is A0C's shape, the middle member on both bias and variance.

The larger lever is not the floor at all. This project's value target is the discounted real-game return, which is the original AlphaZero target - the member they measure as worst on both bias and variance, beaten by all three alternatives on Connect-Four and 6x6 Breakthrough. That is a change to the target in selfplay.cpp and nothing else. Untested here.

Training on every node of the tree against the root alone, search held fixed, is 2157 against 1362 - about 795 Elo.

A correction to an earlier draft of this document. It called an unvisited action TreeStrap's bounded case. It is not. In alpha-beta a cut-off node carries a bound the search produced; here an unvisited action carried a hard zero from network_evaluator.cpp:64, because az::DEATH_RISK_FROM_NETWORK is false. A one-sided loss needs a bound to be one-sided about, and there was none. The 795 Elo does not transfer.

A trap with a number on it. Summing loss over more nodes grows the gradient. They cut the step size ten to twenty fold to match - 1.0e-5 for the root-only methods against 5.0e-7 for the tree-wide one. Any widened label set here has to move the learning rate with it, or the comparison is confounded by an effective step size that grew with the label count.

Two defects this work exposed

An unmeasured risk was indistinguishable from a safe one

An unvisited action's risk was the constant 0.0f - not a bound, not an estimate, and read by any consumer as a measurement saying the move is safe. Result::death_risk is now std::optional<float> per action, and the visit counts are the single authority from which the policy and the coverage flag are derived.

A refusal is not an absence of measurement

The death cap zeroes a refused action's visit count, and the old coverage flag read that zero as unvisited. So a position whose search had measured an action as near-certain death was discarded from training for producing that label - the most informative label on the board. Visits and measurement are separate facts now. DEATH_CAP is false in every non-test configuration, so no measured number moved.

What is still unrun

  • The score half of route one. Does self-play recover from 3.8 with the floor at the root only? One training run answers it, and two iterations kill it if it is going to die.
  • The signal that actually decides. Coverage cannot tell a working route from a broken one - a forced single visit reads as covered either way. The signal is the death loss falling. It sat at 0.0302 and 0.0306 across two iterations, which on two points establishes nothing.
  • The price in play strength. Nothing has been scored on held-out seeds. The measurement noise floor is 198 flips in 1000 games, so nothing under about 3 points is visible at that sample size.
